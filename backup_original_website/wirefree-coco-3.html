WireFREE CoCo 3

Redefining RETRO FREEDOM…..

The Tandy Color Computer 3—fondly known as the CoCo by enthusiasts—has remained a cult favorite in retro computing circles for decades. Powered by the iconic Motorola 6809 processor, often hailed as the most powerful 8-bit CPU ever developed, the CoCo is a staple of vintage computing. However, despite its technological significance, the rigid, space-consuming design has made it difficult for modern users to integrate into their everyday workspaces.

Unlike today’s streamlined technology, the CoCo’s fixed keyboard, separate monitor, and bulky peripherals limit its usability. Moving the computer or adjusting the keyboard for comfort is nearly impossible, making it a desk-bound relic unless users dedicate significant space to accommodate the setup. As a result, countless CoCos remain boxed up in storage, unused for decades.


The WireFree CoCo 3 changes that.

By integrating modern advancements, intelligent product design, and consumer insight, the WireFree CoCo 3 cuts the cords—literally. This innovative approach revolutionizes usability, giving retro enthusiasts the freedom to type anywhere, reposition the setup for comfort, and eliminate the need for special dedicated desk space.

True to Portacoco’s core philosophy, sustainability and reusability remain foundational to the design. Instead of introducing additional waste, the WireFree CoCo 3 builds on existing technological advancements—repurposing modern power solutions and seamlessly integrating into the original CoCo chassis.

Every aspect is meticulously refined:
– 100% reversible installation, ensuring historical preservation
– Precision CAD-designed integration, maintaining the authenticity of the original hardware
– Wireless technology, freeing users from restrictive cables while retaining the retro experience

Combined with other Portacoco innovations—including the Porta CM-8 monitor, PortaAudio Adapter, and the Porta Deluxe 5-in-1 Joystick—the WireFree CoCo 3 makes it possible to retro compute anywhere.

This project embodies problem-solving innovation, blending historical preservation with modern usability, showcasing technical expertise in product development, consumer insight, and strategic design. It redefines retro computing for the future—without sacrificing its past.

OK PRESERVATION • SUSTAINABILITY

Responsible Hardware Architecture

Classic 8-bit computers are irreplaceable historical artifacts. At PortaCoCo, we believe modernization should never compromise original enclosures, nor should it burden the environment with disposable proprietary electronics.

✓
Zero Case Modification: Custom brackets and electrical interposers utilize existing chassis vents and ports without cutting or drilling.
✓
100% Reversible Upgrades: Every PortaCoCo module can be removed at any time, returning the machine to pristine factory collector condition.
✓
Sustainable Battery Reuse: By engineering hardware to run off standard, widely owned power tool batteries (like Ryobi ONE+), we eliminate proprietary battery e-waste and drastically reduce consumer expense.
✓
Efficient DC Regulation: Modern low-heat DC step regulators replace heavy, power-hungry, and volatile vintage AC power transformers.
Precision Fitment: Modular reversible power bracket system.
Founder & Principal Hardware Architect • Agentic AI Systems

Mark Pacan

Founder, Product Development & Hardware Lead • CPG & Agentic AI Strategist

PortaCoCo was established by Mark Pacan as a dedicated hardware innovation venture focused on retro-computing mobility and reversible hardware engineering. Bringing together deep corporate experience across Consumer Insight, Product Development, Information Technology, and Commercial Strategy, Mark dedicated his time to pursuing a vision for retro-tech refinement where beloved computing icons of the past are reborn with contemporary functionality.

Under his direction, PortaCoCo pioneered the Zero Case Modification standard and championed sustainable circular hardware practices—engineering systems that reuse standardized household tool batteries to minimize e-waste, lower consumer acquisition costs, and preserve collector hardware.

To operate with extreme agility, Mark pioneered an AI-first, agentic operational framework throughout the venture. By orchestrating autonomous agentic workflows and leading developer environments—including Claude Code, Google Antigravity, and Cursor—powered by frontier reasoning models such as Claude 3.7 Sonnet (Hybrid Reasoning), Gemini 2.5 Pro, and GPT-4o, he streamlined CAD iteration, embedded firmware synthesis, and cross-border supply chain modeling.

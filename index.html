OK PRESERVATION STANDARD

The Zero Case Modification Principle

Classic 8-bit computers are irreplaceable historical artifacts. At PortaCoCo, we believe modernization should never compromise original enclosures, drill mounting holes, or mar vintage circuit boards.

✓
Non-Destructive Fitment: Custom brackets and electrical interposers utilize existing chassis vents and ports without cutting or drilling.
✓
100% Reversible Upgrades: Every PortaCoCo module can be removed at any time, returning the machine to pristine factory collector condition.
✓
Modern Power Architecture: High-efficiency, low-heat DC regulators and battery management replace heavy, volatile legacy AC transformers.
Precision Fitment: Modular reversible power bracket system.
Founder & Principal Hardware Architect

Mark Pacan

Founder, Product Development & Hardware Lead

PortaCoCo was established by Mark Pacan as a dedicated hardware innovation venture focused on retro-computing mobility and reversible hardware engineering. Bringing together deep corporate experience across Consumer Insight, Product Development, Information Technology, and Commercial Strategy, Mark stepped away in mid-2024 to dedicate himself to something personal: a vision for retro-tech refinement where beloved computing icons of the past are reborn with contemporary functionality.

Under his direction, PortaCoCo pioneered the Zero Case Modification standard—proving that vintage industrial design and contemporary portable technology can merge seamlessly without compromising collector hardware.

To operate with extreme agility, Mark blended modern AI-first workflows throughout the venture. By leveraging agentic AI and developer platforms—including Claude Code, Google Antigravity, Cursor, and frontier reasoning models—he streamlined CAD iteration, electrical architecture, and cross-border supply chain modeling. Grounded in strong commercial discipline, Category Management insight, and Revenue Growth Management (RGM) principles, Mark pairs analytical depth with innovative execution.

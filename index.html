OK PRESERVATION STANDARD

The Zero Case Modification Principle

Vintage 8-bit computers are non-renewable historical artifacts. At PortaCoCo, we believe that bringing classic machines into modern workflows should never require damaging their original enclosures, drilling mounting holes, or permanently modifying vintage circuit boards.

✓
Non-Destructive Fitment: Custom brackets and electrical interposers utilize existing vents, cartridge slots, and expansion ports without cutting.
✓
100% Reversible Upgrades: Any PortaCoCo module can be removed at any time, instantly returning the computer to factory collector condition.
✓
Modern Power Architecture: Efficient low-heat DC regulators and battery management replace heavy, dangerous legacy AC transformers.
Precision Fitment: Modular reversible power bracket system.

Mark Pacan

Founder, Product Development & Hardware Lead

PortaCoCo was established by Mark Pacan as a dedicated hardware innovation venture focused on retro-computing mobility and reversible hardware engineering. Drawing from an extensive executive background spanning Product Development, IT Infrastructure, Consumer Insight, and Operational Strategy, Mark has guided PortaCoCo from initial CAD models to functional working prototypes debuted at national computing conventions.

Under his direction, PortaCoCo pioneered the Zero Case Modification standard—proving that nostalgic industrial design and cutting-edge mobile hardware can blend seamlessly without sacrificing vintage collector integrity.
